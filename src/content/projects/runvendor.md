            <h1>💨 Run! Vendor</h1>
            <p>
                Run! Vendor" is an arcade-style interactive game. Players physically run on a treadmill to
                control the avatar in the endless running game. The treadmill is powered by a Makeymakey
                that
                reads the player's running speed with a hall effect sensor. Whenever the player is running
                too slow, or hits an obstacle, the game ends.
            </p>

            <h2>The Inspiration</h2>
            <p><strong>Experiencing someone's struggle firsthand</strong> </p>
            <!-- <p><a href="#" class="btn btn-primary btn-outline">Get Started</a></p> -->
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-9 animate-box" data-animate-effect="fadeInLeft">
            <p>
                There's an indigenous Cantonese saying in Hong Kong called "走鬼". The literal English
                translation would be "Run! Ghost is here". Local unlicensed food hawkers/vendors refer the
                officials from the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department who patrol on the streets, the
                "ghost", as they will be fined or even be put into jail if they are caught. In recent years,
                street food license is coming to an extinction. By no means, street food vendors are
                welcomed given all the policies that are imposed by the government. They can barely survive.
            </p>
            <p>
                Through this immersive and interactive game experience, we are telling the typical story of
                a food vendor in Hong Kong. Players physically run to survive. We hope players can empathize
                with the poor by experience their struggle firsthand.
            </p>
        </div>
